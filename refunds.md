**REF-1.** Refunds are available for delivered orders within 30 days of the delivery date. Orders that have not been delivered yet cannot be refunded; the customer should wait for delivery or ask for cancellation support.

**REF-2.** Refunds up to 50 EUR for an eligible order can be approved automatically by the support assistant. Refunds above 50 EUR must be reviewed and approved by a human support agent before any money is returned.

**REF-3.** A refund can never exceed the total amount paid for the order, and an order can only be refunded once. Partial refunds are allowed.

**REF-4.** Customers can only request refunds and see details for their own orders. Support staff must never discuss another customer's order.

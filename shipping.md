**SHP-1.** Orders move through the statuses processing, shipped and delivered. Standard delivery takes 2 to 5 working days after the order is shipped.

**SHP-2.** Customers can check the current status and delivery date of their own orders by quoting the order number, for example ORD-1234.

**SHP-3.** If an order has not arrived within 7 working days after shipping, the customer can ask to be connected with a human agent.
